Contact

You can ask a question or report an error or outdated information, and we read every message. We usually reply within a few business days.

Send us a message

The form opens in a new tab and is run by Google Forms. Please don't include medical records, account numbers, or other sensitive details. This site can't advise on a specific family's medical, legal, or financial situation. You can also use this form to make a privacy request (see the privacy policy).

For questions about your parent's Medicare, call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227), visit Medicare.gov, or ask your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) for free one-on-one help. The public services below can help you find local support.

If you need help now

These are public services. They are not operated by this website.

  • If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.
  • If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or visit 988lifeline.org.
  • To find local services for an older adult, use the Eldercare Locator or call 1-800-677-1116.
