Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Family Caregiver Practical Guide handles information. It is written for visitors in the United States. Effective November 10, 2026.

Who we are

This website is published by Family Caregiver Practical Guide. There is no account, membership, or store. The contact page explains how to send us a message.

Information this site collects

You can read every page without telling us your name, email address, or anything else. Pages on this site have no forms; the contact form is a Google Form. If you choose to send a message through it, what you submit is collected and processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and the site owner receives it. Please don't include sensitive personal details. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by a website or by another service the page loads. Two Google services on this site use or may use cookies:

  • Google Analytics 4 loads on every page. It uses cookies to count visits and show which pages are read.
  • Google AdSense is Google's advertising program. When AdSense ads run, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads, including personalized ads based on your visits to this site and other sites.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The guides can still be read with cookies blocked.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-VX24VFJPKZ) to measure visits, such as which pages are read and roughly how visitors arrive. Google Analytics sets cookies on your device and collects information such as your browser type, device, approximate location, and the pages you view. We use these reports only to understand how the site is used. We do not use Google Analytics to collect your name or email address.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. To stop Google Analytics from measuring your visits, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or block or delete cookies in your browser.

Advertising and cookies

Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads on this website. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a person's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to people on this site.

You can read how Google uses data when you use its partners' sites or apps on Google's advertising and cookies policy.

You can opt out of personalized ads from Google at Google Ads Settings. Ads Settings controls ads only, not Google Analytics. You can opt out of some other vendors' cookies through the Digital Advertising Alliance. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking cookies can change how ads work.

Right now no ads are shown. Every page carries a small piece of Google AdSense code that identifies this site's ad account (publisher ID ca-pub-5001125823079992), and the site publishes an ads.txt file that names Google as an authorized ad seller. The code that actually shows ads is not on the pages yet. When ads are turned on, the practices in this section apply, and this policy will be updated.

Affiliate links

Some guides contain links to products on Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, Family Caregiver Practical Guide earns from qualifying purchases. If you buy through one of these links, the site may earn a small commission. You do not pay more. If you click one of these links, Amazon may use cookies to record that you came from this site. Amazon's own privacy notice applies to anything you do on Amazon.

Your privacy rights under US state laws

Some US states, including California, Virginia, and Colorado, have privacy laws that give residents rights over their personal information. Depending on the state, these can include the right to:

  • know what personal information a business collects about you, and get a copy
  • correct personal information that is wrong
  • delete personal information
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, or its use for personalized ads (some state laws call this targeted advertising)

Which rights apply depends on where you live and on each law's own rules. This site does not sell personal information. It does not collect names or contact details, except what you choose to send through the contact form.

To make a request, use the form on the contact page. Say which right you are asking about and which state you live in. To limit personalized ads from Google, use Google Ads Settings. To stop Google Analytics from measuring your visits, use the opt-out add-on described in the Analytics section.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page, GitHub may process technical information such as an IP address and a browser type in order to deliver the page and protect the service. That processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Guides link to public sources such as Medicare, the National Institute on Aging, AARP, and the Eldercare Locator. Those sites have their own privacy practices. This policy covers only Family Caregiver Practical Guide.

Children

The site is written for adults who care for aging parents. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

How to contact us

Use the contact form linked from the contact page. The form is handled by Google Forms (Google Privacy Policy).

This policy is general information about the site. It is not legal advice. See the disclaimer.
