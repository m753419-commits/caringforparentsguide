Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Family Caregiver Practical Guide handles information. It is written for visitors in the United States. Effective October 31, 2026.

Who we are

This website is published by Family Caregiver Practical Guide. There is no account, membership, or store. The contact page will list an email address when the site owner posts one.

Information this site collects

You can read every page without telling us your name, email address, or anything else. If you later email the address on the contact page, we will use that message to reply. Please don't include medical records, Social Security numbers, bank details, or passwords. We do not want that information, and we do not sell personal information.

Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by a website or by another service the page loads. These pages are static files. The site itself does not set cookies for accounts, shopping, or analytics, because it has none of those features.

Advertising and cookies

Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads on this website. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a person's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to people on this site.

You can read how Google uses data when you use its partners' sites or apps on Google's advertising and cookies policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google at Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of some other vendors' cookies through the Digital Advertising Alliance. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking cookies can change how ads work.

Advertising code is not installed on these pages yet. When advertising is added, including advertising from Google, the practices in this section apply.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page, GitHub may process technical information such as an IP address and a browser type in order to deliver the page and protect the service. That processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Guides link to public sources such as Medicare, the National Institute on Aging, AARP, and the Eldercare Locator. Those sites have their own privacy practices. This policy covers only Family Caregiver Practical Guide.

Children

The site is written for adults who care for aging parents. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

How to contact us

Use the contact page. The email address there is still a placeholder, and the owner needs to replace it before privacy questions can be received.

This policy is general information about the site. It is not legal advice. See the disclaimer.
