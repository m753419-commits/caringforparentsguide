How do you check a parent's Medicare coverage, plan, notice, or appeal?

Medicare, the U.S. government health insurance for older people and some younger people with disabilities, raises most of the questions in this group, from a plan or a notice to a hospital stay or a bill.

  • Review a Parent's Medicare Plan in Open Enrollment

    When your parent can change a Medicare plan, and what to check first.

  • Medicare Late Enrollment Penalties (Part B & D)

    How a late sign-up can raise the bill, and which window applies.

  • How to Read a Parent's Medicare Summary Notice

    How to check an Original Medicare (regular Medicare) claims notice that is not a bill.

  • Medicare Advantage Rehab Denial or Care Ending

    Separates a denial by a Medicare Advantage plan (a Medicare-approved plan from a private company) from care that is ending.

  • Hospital Discharge and Medicare Home Health: What to Ask

    What to ask before your parent leaves the hospital, and about care at home.

  • Medicare Hospice: Who Qualifies and How to Start

    Who can get hospice, comfort care near the end of life.

  • Does Medicare Cover Medical Alert Systems?

    Whether Medicare pays for a medical alert button, and what else might.

  • Medigap Plan G vs. Plan N for a Parent: Questions to Compare

    Which questions to ask about Medigap Plan G and Plan N (private policies that help pay some Original Medicare costs).

  • Missed Medigap Open Enrollment: What to Check Next

    What to check after that one-time Medigap sign-up period, and why the fall sign-up period is different.

  • Request a Lower Medicare IRMAA With SSA-44

    How a retired parent can ask Social Security (the federal agency that pays retirement and disability benefits) to review an extra monthly Medicare charge with a life-change form.

How can a family pay for care, choose a nursing home, or get paid to give it?

Prices, choosing a nursing home, taxes, and pay for a family caregiver are all here, along with Medicaid, a joint federal and state program that helps with health costs for people with limited income and resources.

  • How Much Does It Cost to Care for an Aging Parent?

    How much care can cost, and where to start a monthly budget.

  • Medicaid 5-Year Look-Back for a Parent's Nursing Home

    How Medicaid can help pay for a nursing home, and how gifts can delay help.

  • Nursing Home Comparison and Visit Checklist

    How to compare nursing homes and what to ask on a visit.

  • How to Get Paid to Care for a Parent (Medicaid, VA & More)

    Whether Medicaid or another program can pay you to care for a parent.

  • Can I Claim My Parent as a Dependent? (2026 Tax Year)

    Whether you can list a parent on your tax return.

How do families share the work, the papers, and a break?

This group is for the people giving the care: burnout, free support, a short break, time off work, sharing tasks with siblings, and legal papers.

  • Caregiver Burnout: Warning Signs and What to Do

    Lists warning signs of caregiver burnout, which means feeling worn out from care.

  • Free Help for Family Caregivers: Support Groups

    Where a family caregiver can look for help, including support groups.

  • Respite Care for Family Caregivers: How to Find It

    How to find respite care, a short break while someone else stays with your parent.

  • Caring for a Parent While Working: FMLA Basics

    Explains FMLA (the Family and Medical Leave Act): unpaid leave that can protect your job if you qualify.

  • How to Split Caregiving Fairly Among Siblings

    How siblings can share tasks and money without matching hours.

  • Guardianship vs. Power of Attorney: What to Know

    Compares a signed paper with a court order used when your parent cannot decide.

  • Documents You Need for an Aging Parent: Checklist

    Which papers to gather, starting with what your parent already has.
